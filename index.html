🌊 Bay Tides

Three bays, three NOAA stations. For the launch, the crab pots, the clam beds or a beach hour, here is the next turn of the tide at Garibaldi (Tillamook Bay), Netarts Bay and Nehalem Bay.

Loading NOAA tide predictions…

Tide predictions from NOAA Tides & Currents (Garibaldi 9437540, Netarts Bay 9437262, Nehalem 9437908), in feet above mean lower low water. Wind and river flow push real water off the prediction — a flooding river runs the bay higher — so treat the times as the plan, not a promise. Nestucca Bay has no NOAA prediction station of its own; the Pacific City launch works off the Garibaldi pattern.

🦀 Crabbing Index — Tillamook Bay

When should you throw the pots in this week? This index scores each of the next seven days for crabbing from a boat in Tillamook Bay — the slack-tide window, the wind, the rain, how much fresh water the bay’s five rivers are pushing in, and the season — and names the best hours of every day. Slack water is when crab walk and feed (ODFW’s own guidance); the classic bay plan is to set pots an hour or so before high slack and fish through the turn. Scored at Garibaldi, the bay’s reference harbor.

Scoring the next seven days on the bay…

The rules, in short (ODFW)

Season: Tillamook Bay is open for Dungeness crab all year, 24 hours a day. The Oct 16 – Nov 30 closure is ocean waters only — it does not apply inside the bay.

License: everyone 12 and older needs an Oregon shellfish license to crab.

Limit: 12 Dungeness crab per person per day — males only. Females and undersized crab go back.

Size: 5¾ inches minimum, measured straight across the back (caliper), in front of the points.

Gear: up to 3 pots, rings or baited lines per person. Pot buoys must be marked with your name, plus your address, phone number, ODFW ID or vessel ID.

The index is an estimate, built live from NOAA tide predictions, the National Weather Service forecast and USGS river gauges — the bay always has the last word. The rules above are a summary; the final word is ODFW’s shellfish regulations, and shellfish safety closures sit with the Oregon Department of Agriculture (hotline 1-800-448-2474) — check both the day you go.

🏍️ OHV — Ride Areas

This county is Oregon's OHV backyard: three riding areas in the Tillamook State Forest off OR-6, plus the Sand Lake dunes. The forest's three areas together hold more than 250 miles of trails (ODF's figure, all three combined). Every staging area below — plus Rogers Camp at the OR-6 summit — is pinned and labeled on the Travel map; tap a pin for trail classes, permits and seasons.

Browns Camp OHV Area

Tillamook State Forest · OR-6 · ODF

Motorcycle, quad and 4WD trails from easy to challenging, with a campground and staging area; day use is free unless you take a campsite. The campground moves to reservation-only for 2026, and the forest's seasonal OHV campgrounds close November 2 for winter (ODF).

📍 See it in Google Maps

Jordan Creek OHV Area

Tillamook State Forest · ODF

Motorcycle, quad and 4WD trails with campground and staging; part of the same 250+ mile forest system. Its developed campground is in the seasonal group that closes November 2.

📍 See it in Google Maps

Diamond Mill OHV Area

Tillamook State Forest · ODF

The different one: mostly single-track trails for motorcycles only — technical riding, not a quad or 4WD area. Campground and staging on site.

📍 See it in Google Maps

Sand Lake Recreation Area

Near Pacific City · Siuslaw National Forest

1,076 acres of open sand dunes between Cape Lookout and Cape Kiwanda, with three campgrounds, dispersed camping and a day-use staging area, open year-round. A fee applies at the campgrounds, the day-use lot and on the open sand itself.

📍 See it in Google Maps

Check before you go: Tillamook State Forest blog — ODF's running status log · ODF fire restrictions & closures map · ODF campground status table

Fire season rewrites the rules: at High fire danger, OHV use runs only before 1 PM and after 8 PM; at Extreme, off-road use is restricted. Every rider needs an Oregon ATV operating permit — $10, good for two years — from the Oregon State Parks store or an agent. Side-by-sides (Class IV) are allowed only on signed 4WD trails and gravel roads inside the forest's OHV areas.

🏖️ Parks, Beaches & Trails

Three capes, a string of state parks, and the county's own parks and trails. Statuses below are what the official pages said when this page was built — each entry links to its own page, which is always the final word.

Cape Lookout State Park

Netarts · Oregon State Parks

Closed for construction: day-use, the campground and the hiker/biker camp closed August 3, 2026 for GO Bond construction, per the park's own page — check there before you go. The Cape Lookout trail (about 5 miles round trip, muddy and rocky) starts here.

Cape Meares State Scenic Viewpoint & Lighthouse

Cape Meares · Oregon State Parks

The short lighthouse on the headland west of Tillamook — the tower is open Memorial Day to September 30; the grounds, viewpoints and the Octopus Tree are open year-round.

Cape Kiwanda State Natural Area

Pacific City · Oregon State Parks

The sandstone cape and Haystack Rock, above the dory fleet's beach. Pacific City is home port to more than 300 dory boats, and access for their launching is protected here.

Bob Straub State Park

Pacific City · Oregon State Parks

The quiet end of Pacific City: beach access and the Nestucca River mouth, across from the cape. The park page carries a one-lane advisory on Sandlake Road.

Oswald West State Park

Arch Cape · Oregon State Parks

Short Sand Beach and the rainforest trails of the county's north end — the surfers' cove below Neahkahnie Mountain.

Nehalem Bay State Park

Manzanita · Oregon State Parks

A spit between ocean and bay: big campground (265 electrical sites after its Fall 2025 GO Bond work), beach on one side, the bay boat ramp on the other. Snowy plover management area rules apply March 15 – September 15.

Barview Jetty County Park

Rockaway Beach / Garibaldi · Tillamook County Parks

The county park at the mouth of Tillamook Bay — campground, jetty fishing and the bay's front door. Open year-round; the west half closes October – April.

Sitka Sedge State Natural Area

Near Pacific City · Oregon State Parks

357 quiet acres of estuary, marsh and forest trails by Sand Lake — the walker's park.

Munson Creek Falls State Natural Site

South of Tillamook · Oregon State Parks

At 319 feet, the tallest waterfall in the Coast Range, down a quarter-mile trail through old growth. Open for day use year-round; watch Munson Creek for spawning salmon in late fall and winter.

Kilchis Point Reserve

Bay City · Tillamook County Pioneer Museum

200 acres where the Kilchis River meets the bay — two-plus miles of trails, three of them interpretive, through the county's first pioneer settlement site.

Tillamook Forest Center

45500 Wilson River Hwy (OR-6) · ODF

The forest's own visitor center — exhibits, the lookout tower replica and the Wilson River at the door. Posted hours: Wednesday – Sunday, 10 AM – 4 PM in spring, summer and fall; closed in winter. $5 suggested donation.

📞 (503) 815-6800

Three Capes Scenic Loop: the loop is driveable again — the Cape Meares Loop Road, closed by a slide in 2013, reopened on a new inland alignment after a decade away. Old "loop closed" maps still circulating online predate the reopening. Any new closure posts on the county's Emergency Management page.

Four days every August at the fairgrounds in Tillamook — livestock, grandstand shows and the Pig-N-Ford races, a fair tradition for over 40 years; the fair itself dates to 1891. 🔔 The County Watch

Not watching

Everything else on this page waits for you to come and look. The watch doesn't. Leave this page open — in a tab, installed on your home screen, or up on the wall — and it checks the National Weather Service and the county’s roads every two minutes. If a new warning posts, or a closure lands on US-101 or OR-6 while you're not looking, it tells you: a notification on this device where the browser offers them, a line in the log below, and — if you tick the box — it says new warnings out loud.

Setting up the watch…

    Honest limits: the watch only runs while this page is open on this device — closing the tab stops it, and a phone may pause a background tab. It's a convenience, never a replacement for the National Weather Service, NOAA Weather Radio, or Tillamook County emergency alerts.

    🌲 About Tillamook County Live

    Tillamook County Live is one live look at Tillamook County, Oregon — US-101 and OR-6, the Wilson, Trask, Nehalem and Nestucca rivers and their flood stages, tides on the county’s three bays, the Tillamook Bay bar report, weather alerts, and what’s on around the county — assembled from official public sources and refreshed all day. It is built and run by Nomad Development Group, a small Oregon studio, on free public data and free hosting, which is how a page like this can exist for a county of 32,000 at all. It is the younger sibling of Columbia County Live, one county north.

    Why it’s free — and what stays free. The core of this page is public-safety data: weather alerts, river levels and flood stages, road conditions, bar conditions. That core is free, carries no sponsor, and stays that way — it will never sit behind a paywall. That promise is the reason the page exists. The Community Help section keeps that same promise: the county’s free help — food pantries, crisis lines, help with rent and utility bills, support for families and veterans — gathered in one place, free to everyone who needs it.

    How this page is paid for

    Right now, honestly: it isn’t — it runs on the same free public feeds and free hosting as its sibling, and the operator’s time. There is no sponsor program on this page yet. When local support opens, it will be announced here first, and it will never touch the free safety core above.

    ✉️ Write to the site

    Spotted a wrong number, a stale hour, or a listing that should be here? That button opens an email to the person who runs this page. Corrections from the county are how a page like this stays worth trusting — writing costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

    🤝 Community Help

    Free food, crisis lines, help with rent and power bills, health care that slides to what you can pay, support for families, seniors, veterans and recovery — the help this county already offers, gathered in one place. Most of it is free; where a service charges on a sliding scale, its listing says so. Tap any phone number to call. Every listing here was checked against the service’s own website or official listing.

    In an emergency, call 911.

    🍞 Food — free pantries & meals

    Ten food sources from Wheeler to Hebo, every one checked against its own listing. Hours shift around holidays — when a trip depends on it, call first.

    Oregon Food Bank — Tillamook County

    Countywide network hub

    The warehouse that stocks this county’s food pantries. It is a distribution hub and does not hand food to individuals directly — the pantries below are where to go; this is the number for donations, volunteering and finding the nearest distribution.

    📞 (503) 842-3154

    North County Food Bank

    Wheeler · Tuesdays 12 – 3 PM

    Free groceries for north county households, every Tuesday afternoon.

    📞 (503) 368-7724278 Rowe St, Wheeler

    Nehalem Bay Community Services

    Nehalem · Food pantry Mon / Fri / Sat 10 AM – 2 PM, Wed 1 – 5 PM

    Food pantry plus senior meals on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Use this number — it is the one on the organization’s own site; directories list others.

    📞 (503) 368-5612

    Tillamook Food Pantry

    Tillamook · Mondays 4 – 6 PM

    Free groceries Monday evenings in Tillamook.

    📞 (503) 815-38803808 12th St, Tillamook

    God's Lighthouse

    Garibaldi · Fridays 10 AM – 12 PM, twice a month

    Free food distribution in Garibaldi on two Fridays a month — call ahead to confirm which Fridays.

    📞 (503) 322-3501

    Seventh-day Adventist Community Services

    Tillamook · Tue / Thu 10 AM – 2 PM

    Food and clothing help in Tillamook. This center is also the local Salvation Army service agency — there is no separate Salvation Army office in the county.

    📞 (503) 842-4794

    Nestucca Pass It On

    Beaver · Tue 10 AM – 12 PM, Thu 4:30 – 6 PM

    South county food pantry in Beaver, run by Nestucca Pass It On Ministries.

    📞 (503) 398-2803

    Hebo Food Pantry

    Hebo · Last Saturday of the month, 12 – 2 PM

    Monthly food distribution for the Hebo and south county area.

    📞 (503) 392-3585

    Meals for Seniors

    Rockaway Beach · Lunch Mon / Wed / Fri

    Hot lunch for seniors in Rockaway Beach, three days a week. A $4 donation is requested — no one is turned away for not paying.

    📞 (503) 317-8967

    First Christian Church — The Dining Room

    Tillamook · Tue / Thu 4 – 5:30 PM

    Free hot evening meals in Tillamook, Tuesdays and Thursdays. Just show up.

    📞 (503) 842-6213

    🆘 Crisis & safety

    If someone is in danger right now, call 911 first. These lines are for everything around that moment — before, after, and when you are not sure.

    Tides of Change

    Domestic violence & sexual assault support · 24/7 helpline

    Tillamook County’s domestic violence and sexual assault program: advocacy, safety planning and shelter referrals, any hour. No address is published for this service — by design, for safety.

    📞 (503) 842-9486📞 (800) 992-1679

    988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

    Call or text 988 · 24/7

    Free, confidential support for anyone in emotional distress or a mental health crisis — you do not have to be suicidal to call.

    📞 988

    Tillamook Family Counseling Center — county crisis line

    Tillamook · 24/7 crisis line

    The county’s behavioral health crisis line, answered around the clock. TFCC also runs the county’s recovery and counseling services (see Veterans, Legal & Recovery).

    📞 (503) 842-8201📞 1-800-962-2851

    Tillamook County Sheriff — non-emergency dispatch

    Countywide · 24/7

    For anything urgent but not an emergency: suspicious activity, welfare checks, reporting after the fact. Emergencies are always 911.

    📞 (503) 815-1911Main office: 5995 Long Prairie Rd, Tillamook · (503) 842-2561

    💵 Money & housing help

    Rent, power bills, shelter and benefits. Start with CARE for housing money and 211 when you do not know where to start.

    CARE Inc.

    Tillamook · Emergency & rental assistance, housing help, peer support

    The county’s front door for housing help: emergency assistance, rental assistance and housing programs, plus peer support. (Its 10-unit microshelter village is still listed as coming soon on CARE’s own site — not open yet, so it is not presented as shelter here.)

    📞 (503) 842-52612310 1st St, Tillamook

    Helping Hands — Tillamook Hope Center

    Tillamook · Shelter & transitional housing

    Shelter and transitional housing help. One honest limit, from Helping Hands’ own site: the Hope Center does not provide rental assistance — for rent help, call CARE above.

    📞 (503) 354-8014

    Tillamook PUD — Customer Assistance Program (CAP)

    Countywide · Help with power bills

    Income-qualified help with Tillamook PUD electric bills. Qualification runs through CARE (above) — the PUD’s CAP page explains the steps. After-hours power line below is for outages and emergencies.

    📞 (503) 842-2535📞 After hours: (503) 842-21221115 Pacific Ave, Tillamook

    211info

    Dial 211 · Statewide, free

    One free call connects you to local help — food, housing, bills, childcare, almost anything. Text your ZIP code to 898211 if you would rather text.

    📞 211📞 (866) 698-6155

    Oregon DHS — Tillamook office

    Tillamook · SNAP, cash assistance, Oregon Health Plan

    The state benefits office for the county: food benefits (SNAP), TANF and medical coverage applications and help.

    📞 (503) 842-44534670 3rd St, Tillamook

    Tillamook County Community Health Centers (TCCHC)

    Tillamook · County health clinics & dental

    The county health department’s clinics — primary care and dental on a sliding fee scale. The health department and TCCHC are the same service, listed once here.

    📞 (503) 842-3900📞 Dental: (503) 842-2356

    WIC — Women, Infants & Children

    Tillamook · Food benefits for pregnant women, new moms & kids under 5

    Nutrition benefits, breastfeeding support and health screening for pregnant and postpartum women and children under five. Call or text Allison to apply.

    📞 (503) 842-39432111 8th St, Tillamook

    Adventist Health Tillamook

    Tillamook · Hospital · Emergency room open 24 hours

    The county hospital: 24-hour emergency department (Level 4 trauma center), clinics and surgery.

    📞 (503) 842-44441000 Third St, Tillamook

    Nehalem Bay Health Center & Pharmacy

    Wheeler · Sliding-fee clinic & pharmacy

    North county’s clinic (formerly Rinehart Clinic) — medical care and pharmacy on a sliding fee scale; per its own site, no one is denied care for inability to pay.

    📞 1-800-368-5182885 Nehalem Blvd, Wheeler

    NorthWest Senior & Disability Services — Tillamook office

    Tillamook · Seniors & people with disabilities

    Help with aging and disability services, benefits, in-home care and caregiver support. The ADRC line (second number) is the statewide Aging & Disability Resource Connection.

    📞 (503) 842-2770📞 ADRC: 866-206-47995010 E. Third St, Tillamook

    🎖️ Veterans, legal & recovery

    Veterans’ benefits help, free civil legal aid, and recovery services.

    Tillamook County Veterans' Services

    Tillamook · Mon – Fri, 8 AM – 5 PM

    Free help for veterans and their families with VA claims, benefits and records — the county service officer works for veterans, not the VA.

    📞 (503) 842-4358201 Laurel Ave, Room 12, Tillamook

    Veterans Crisis Line

    Call 988, then press 1 · Text 838255 · 24/7

    For veterans in crisis and the people who love them. Confidential, answered by people trained to help veterans.

    📞 988

    Oregon Law Center — Hillsboro office

    Serves Tillamook County · Free civil legal aid

    Free legal help for low-income Oregonians on civil matters — housing and eviction defense, benefits, family safety. The Hillsboro regional office covers Tillamook County. Tenant hotline first number; public benefits line second.

    📞 (503) 648-7723📞 Benefits: 1-800-520-5292

    Tillamook Family Counseling Center — recovery services

    Tillamook · Substance-use recovery & behavioral health

    The county’s recovery and behavioral health provider — assessment, treatment and peer support. The crisis line (second listing under Crisis & safety) is the same agency, answered 24/7.

    📞 (503) 842-8201

    CARE Inc. — peer support

    Tillamook · Peer support & recovery community

    CARE (listed under Money & housing for rent and housing help) also runs peer support — people with lived experience helping people through it. Same number.

    📞 (503) 842-5261

    📱 Put Tillamook County Live on your home screen

    First — you're inside Facebook's browser, and it can't add home-screen apps. Tap the three dots at the top corner and choose Open in external browser or Open in Chrome. Then follow the steps below.

    First — open this page in Safari. You're inside another app's browser, and adding it from here looks like it worked, but the icon never appears — that's an Apple rule, not you doing it wrong. Tap the share icon and choose Open in Safari, then follow the steps below.

    On iPhone & iPad (in Safari)

    1. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen.
    2. Scroll the list that pops up and tap Add to Home Screen.
    3. Tap Add in the top corner. Done — the county is now one tap away.

    On Android

    In Chrome:

    1. Tap ⋮ at the top-right corner.
    2. Tap Install app (some phones say Add to Home screen).
    3. Tap Install or Add to confirm.

    In Samsung Internet: tap the menu (☰), then Add page to → Home screen.

    In Firefox: tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen.

    On a computer

    In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar — or just bookmark this page (Ctrl/Cmd + D). It updates itself all day.

    Different phone or browser? See the other instructions

    Don't see the icon? Check your last home screen page — new icons often land there. On iPhone, you can also swipe left to the App Library and search “Tillamook”.
